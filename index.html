03ExpertiseSix disciplines

Six disciplines,
one integrated view.

Hover a discipline to explore it. Each one is a point of contact between product, production and market.

  • Support in the development of footwear products, from the initial idea to the definition of their characteristics, coordinating the different figures involved. Range architecture, last and material choices, technical briefs and sampling milestones are kept aligned with what the company can actually produce and sell.

    • Range planning
    • Technical briefs
    • Sampling calendar

  • Analysis of market opportunities, positioning, business evolution and strategic support for companies. The aim is a clear direction: where to play, what to prioritise, what to leave out — translated into plans a product, sales and operations team can all execute.

    • Market opportunity
    • Growth roadmap
    • Priorities & trade-offs

  • Creation and development of commercial relationships, analysis of opportunities and support for the growth of B2B activities. From account mapping to proposition, pricing corridors and showroom calendars, the commercial side is built together with the product, not after it.

    • Account mapping
    • B2B proposition
    • Pricing corridors

  • Coordination between companies, partners, suppliers and teams involved in product development and management. One shared calendar, one shared language: fewer misunderstandings between design, technical office, production and sales.

    • Supplier alignment
    • Milestones & gates
    • Cross-team routines

  • Support in defining positioning, product identity, target and brand perception. Products are given a clear role within the brand — icon, core or statement — so that the collection reads as a coherent proposition on the shelf and online.

    • Positioning
    • Product roles
    • Target & perception

  • Analysis of trends, the footwear market, opportunities and the evolution of purchasing behaviours. Signals from retail, materials and consumers are read together and distilled into briefs that product and commercial teams can act on.

    • Trend reading
    • Assortment gaps
    • Buying behaviour
